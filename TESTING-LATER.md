# Testing for 1.15.0

Every feature added on `capcut-pro`, what its automated tests cover, and
what needs a real check by the owner. Tests are run in the final pass only.

| Feature | Tests (file › what) | Needs a real check by you |
|---|---|---|
| A1 Keyframes | VideoKeyframesTests › easing curves, values between keys, editing at the playhead (key vs base), diamond toggles a group, ⌥K / ◀ ▶, keys through trim and split, keyed opacity in exported pixels, keyed volume in exported sound, format 4 | Drag an item on the player with keys on: it should add a key at the playhead and the motion should look smooth with each easing |
| A2 Blend, Cutout, Masks | VideoItemLookTests › multiply/screen pixels and only where the item is, chroma key takes green out and keeps the subject, every mask shape keeps/hides the right pixels, invert, remove background runs, each edit one undo step, everything starts off, locked track refuses | Remove background on a real person (camera clip): edge quality, and whether playback keeps up; chroma key on a real green-screen clip |
| A3 Animations | VideoAnimationsTests › every preset starts away and ends in place, in/out/combo over an item, slide and blur values, fade-in in exported pixels, the command is one step and None takes it off | Look at each preset on the player: they should feel smooth at 0.5 s |
| A4 Speed | VideoSpeedTests › curve baked into pieces of one group, steady speed restores one clip, presets stay in range, no transitions inside a curve, Keep pitch on/off measured in exported sound (440 vs 880 Hz at 2×), 100× and 0.1× lengths, each command one step, custom-curve drag one step, locked refuses | Listen to Keep pitch off in the preview, and drag the curve points: playback should follow without stutter |
| A5 Audio | VideoAudioToolsTests › beats found on a synthetic click track (and none in silence), Normalize raises a soft tone and lowers a loud one, volume keys + beats + normalize are one step each, beats are snap targets, locked refuses, keyed volume and a video's sound fade measured in the export | Detect beats on real music (does it land on the beat you hear?); Normalize on a real voice |
| A6 Text clips | VideoTextClipsTests › every font is a macOS font and unknown fonts fall back, colour and background in pixels, size and letter spacing change the shape, a text clip in exported pixels, ⌥⇧T adds it on its own track at the playhead (one step), typing is one step, a hand-set size keeps its proportion, undo, locked refuses, 1.14 items write no text key, format 4 | Type in each font with bold/italic/underline, stroke, shadow and background on the player; check the box on the player hugs the text. In Creative Handoff / FCP / Premiere, text clips are part of the rendered video, not a separate element |
| A7 Motion tracking | VideoMotionTrackingTests › offsets between points, hold before/after, shift on trim/split, drawn where it follows, Vision follows a synthetic moving checked square (dx within 5% of the frame, no drift up/down), Track through the editor moves a text clip with the square in one undo step, Stop following is one step, locked refuses, format 4 | Track a real moving thing (a face, a car, the pointer) with a sticker or text: does it stay on it, and how long does tracking take on a 30 s clip? If it drifts or loses track on ordinary footage, move it to next update |
